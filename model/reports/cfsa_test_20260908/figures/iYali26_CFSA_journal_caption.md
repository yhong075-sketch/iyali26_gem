**Figure X. Comparative flux sampling of iYali26 under growth and lipid-demand constraints.** The provisional reference was sampled under SD-Leu/PO1f conditions (500 retained draws per scenario; one chain; thinning interval, 100; no additional burn-in removal). Minimum growth was 10% of the original wild-type maximum; growing and producing scenarios used 90% optimality constraints. **a,** Growth versus mixed-lipid pseudopool demand; diamonds indicate arithmetic means. **b,** Sum of absolute net reaction fluxes, Σ|v|. Boxes show the interquartile range, lines the medians, whiskers the full range and diamonds the arithmetic means. Dashed segments denote scenario-specific pFBA forward-plus-reverse flux caps, which also bound the net sums; no cap applies to the producing scenario. Panel a's horizontal axis and panel b's vertical axis are logarithmic. **c,** Empirical cumulative distributions of lag-1 Pearson autocorrelation for 1,369 varying reactions per scenario (sample peak-to-peak range >10⁻⁷). Lipid demand and flux sums use model reaction scales, not TAG yields or enzyme costs. High lipid demand is imposed by the production constraint. All saved net samples passed previously audited feasibility checks at 10⁻⁷ tolerance; original split-variable trajectories were not saved. The draws remain strongly correlated, and adequate mixing, convergence and engineering-target validity have not been established.
