# iYali26 CFSA 科研展示图

**中文图注。** 暂定参考模型在 SD-Leu／PO1f 配置下的 CFSA 小规模试验。A，三种约束条件：高生长、慢生长对照及高产物；生长下限均不低于原 WT 最大生长的10%，高生长和生产约束采用90%最优性。前两组各有基于本组pFBA的总通量上限，生产组无该上限。B，全部1,500个保留样本的生长与混合脂质需求通量，菱形为算术均值，横轴为对数坐标。生产组的高脂质需求由约束指定，不能解释成干预增产证据。C，每个样本的净反应通量绝对值之和；箱体表示25%-75%分位数，须线覆盖全样本范围，菱形和文字为算术均值，纵轴为对数坐标。虚线为保存的pFBA正反变量总量上限，是净L1投影可行性检查的对应上限，不是已保存的正反变量轨迹。通量总和采用模型反应尺度，不应解释为实测总代谢速率或蛋白成本。D，1,369条变化反应的滞后1阶Pearson自相关经验累积分布；变化定义为500个样本的峰峰值大于1e-7。每组一个链，thinning=100，未额外丢弃burn-in；0.9虚线仅作描述性参考。高自相关说明样本相关，但本身不证明非平稳。全部样本在1e-7容差下满足已核查的净通量约束；充分混合、收敛和工程靶点有效性均尚未建立。总脂质需求仅在一次性内存测试模型中加入，不是纯TAG产率或实测分泌量。

**English legend.** Comparative flux sampling pilot using the provisional iYali26 reference under SD-Leu/PO1f conditions. A, three constrained scenarios: growing, slow growing and producing, with a minimum growth of 10% of the original WT maximum. Growing and producing use 90% optimality constraints. Separate pFBA-based total-flux caps apply to the two growth scenarios but not to the production scenario. B, all 1,500 retained samples; diamonds indicate arithmetic means and the horizontal axis is logarithmic. High lipid demand is imposed by the production constraint rather than demonstrating an intervention benefit. C, the sum of absolute net reaction fluxes per sample; boxes show the interquartile range, whiskers the full range, and diamonds and labels the arithmetic means. The vertical axis is logarithmic. Dashed segments indicate the recorded pFBA caps on forward-plus-reverse variables, used here to check net-flux projection feasibility; original split-variable trajectories were not saved. The sum uses model reaction scales and is not a measured total metabolic rate or protein cost. D, empirical cumulative distributions of lag-1 Pearson autocorrelation across 1,369 varying reactions, defined by a sample peak-to-peak range above 1e-7. Each scenario contains one chain with 500 retained samples, thinning=100 and no additional burn-in removal. The 0.9 line is descriptive, not a convergence threshold. High autocorrelation indicates dependent draws but does not itself establish nonstationarity. All samples satisfy the reconstructed net-flux constraints at a tolerance of 1e-7. Adequate mixing, convergence and engineering-target validity remain unestablished. Mixed-lipid demand was added only to a disposable test model and does not represent a pure-TAG yield or measured secretion.
