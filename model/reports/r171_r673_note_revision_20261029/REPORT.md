## 6. Limits and open wording points

- **Found by this round's audit, not changed** (changing them would change the wording the user approved):
  - "with no UniProt location" for YHR020W is exact only for UniProt's Subcellular location section. P38708 carries
    the GO CC term cytoplasm (IEA:InterPro, verified by the audit). "no UniProt subcellular-location annotation"
    would be precise.
  - "the assignment rests on the two predictors and synteny" may overstate (inferred). The "ancestral locus Anc_x"
    tags come from the same homology annotation (YGOB loci of the *S. cerevisiae* homologs; that *Y. lipolytica* is
    not in YGOB is established / uncited). So they are not independent synteny evidence. "rests on the two
    predictors; the ancestral-locus tags come from the same homology annotation" would be accurate. The same
    "synteny" wording appears in the R673 data's `evidence_tier` note and in earlier reports.
- The R171 note still does not list the evidence against removal; that evidence is in the R171 REPORT §1.
- No Escher figure: no flux changes.
