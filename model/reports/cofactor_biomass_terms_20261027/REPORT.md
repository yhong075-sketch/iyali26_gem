## 1. What changed and why

The Ponce-de-León 2013 census of candidate a1237821
([REPORT.md](../gap_metabolites_ponce2013_20261026/REPORT.md) section 5) found 72 blocked reactions that are
*coenzyme pseudo-gaps*: a cofactor that is synthesised but never drained, so its biosynthesis cannot carry flux.
The paper's remedy is a growth-dilution (biomass) term for the cofactor; `biomass_C` already had such terms
for TDP and heme a, both at yeast-GEM values (STATE.md 2026-10-22). This task adds the six cofactors that
yeast-GEM 9.1.1 prices in its cofactor pseudoreaction `r_4598` and the census tested by drains.

## 2. Code logic

- **Data:** [cofactor_biomass_terms.json](../../curation/biomass/cofactor_biomass_terms.json), generated by
  [scripts/make_curation_file.py](scripts/make_curation_file.py) from before.json: the six species with name,
  formula, charge, compartment and reaction list, the coefficients and their yeast-GEM provenance, the identity
  checks, the authorization, and the single accepted before-state (`a1237821_biomass_C`: the exact 55-term
  stoichiometry, bounds and note keys of a1237821's `biomass_C`).
- **Step:** `platform/scripts/gem_annotate/cofactor_biomass_terms.py`, `apply_cofactor_biomass_terms()`, modelled
  on `heme_iron_biomass.py`. It raises, without editing, when the data file's scope is not the reviewed one
  (ids, status, the six terms, positive coefficients, the before-state id and shape), the objective is not
  `biomass_C` alone, a species is missing or its name/formula/charge/compartment or reaction context differs,
  `biomass_C` (minus the six terms) differs from the before-state in stoichiometry, bounds or note keys, or the
  terms are partly present, present with other coefficients, or present without the curation's notes. A rerun
  returns `already_correct`. The `baseline_model_sha256` field is provenance only: a file hash cannot be
  checked against an in-memory model mid-build; the before-state comparison is the executable staleness guard
  (review finding 2, section 7).
- **Flag:** `--cofactor-biomass-terms` (cli.py, main.py), requires `--lipoylation-module` (the last earlier step
  that edits `biomass_C`) and runs after `--acyl-acp-chemistry`, as the last step that edits `biomass_C`, so
  the heme/iron guard's reviewed before-states are untouched. (The help text says "runs last"; the three
  CoQ GPR-only steps that follow it in main.py do not touch `biomass_C` and are not in the a1237821 argv.) It is recorded in the `.build.json` options.
- **Test:** `platform/tests/test_cofactor_biomass_terms.py` (5 tests, 23 subtests) on the a1237821 file in
  memory: spec/task consistency, 12 fail-closed model states, 11 rejected specs (each leaving the model
  untouched), the exact edit and SBML round trip, the flag coupling.

## 7. Code review (fresh read-only reviewer, before the final build)

1. **Bug (low, fixed):** the before-state `id` was read only after the edit, so a spec without it would edit the
   model and then raise `KeyError`. The scope guard now pins `id == "a1237821_biomass_C"`; two new test cases
   (missing and wrong id) and an untouched-model assertion for every rejected spec were added.
2. **Scope/test (low, accepted):** the plan's "stale SHA" wording; the SHA field is provenance only (section 2).
3. **Note (inferred):** the flag requires only `--lipoylation-module`, so `--lipoylation-module
   --cofactor-biomass-terms` without `--acyl-acp-chemistry` would most likely pass the guard and give an
   unreviewed combination. Left as is: it matches the authorization, and the acyl-ACP step does not touch
   `biomass_C` or the six species.

Tests: full suite before 264 passed / 11 failed / 31 skipped / 18 errors (identical to the 2026-10-23 run; the
vacuole, gem_review literature and sn-12 failures pre-date this task); with the pre-review module 269 passed
(the five new tests) with the same failures; the final suite on the post-review code
([logs/pytest_after.log](logs/pytest_after.log)): 269 passed / 11 failed / 31 skipped / 18 errors, 704
subtests, identical to the baseline test by test plus the five new passes.

## 8. Findings outside the scope (not changed)

- **The census closure checks' "no energy-generating cycle" values are masked infeasibles** (verified: the census
  `closure_check.py` on the Kennedy case returns a NaN objective, GLPK status "undefined" in this session's rerun
  and "infeasible" in the auditor's, and writes `0.0 if v != v`). The
  no-uptake LP is infeasible on this candidate because of the `xMAINTENANCE` lower bound; with it relaxed the
  maximum is 0 for the biomass change here, but the five census closure cases were not re-checked. This affects
  the census report of 2026-10-26 (another session's uncommitted deliverable; not edited here).
- CoA `m69` and NAD `m122` carry class-level ChEBI / MetaNetX ids ("coenzyme", "NAD unspecified"); NADP `m41`'s
  name says C21H28 but its formula is C21H29 (the KEGG value).
- Medium folate is a dead end (R849 → `m1051[C_cy]` only); the model cannot use folate from YNB.
- The riboflavin de novo genes are consensus-essential although the screens' YNB contains riboflavin; the
  model keeps them non-essential on the Task A medium. An experimental-list question.
- The remaining 21 cofactor pseudo-gap reactions (FMN, PLP, dolichyl-P, siroheme, biotin, pantetheine-4'-P)
  and the mitochondrial copies stay blocked; NADH, NADPH and glutathione terms of r_4598 were not added.
