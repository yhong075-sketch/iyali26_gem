Audit date: 2026-09-11. Reviewer: `/root/dipeptidyl_gene_audit`. Scope: one bounded read-only review of two database candidates, two historical institution reports, and the cited FEMS work. No BLAST, AlphaFold, optimization, model/medium/GPR edits, or experimental execution.
