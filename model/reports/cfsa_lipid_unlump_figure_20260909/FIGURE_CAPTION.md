**Figure. CFSA sampling of the charge-corrected iYali26 lipid-unlump candidate.** Growing, Slow growth and Producing conditions are shown in blue, ochre and green, respectively. (a) Total TAG mass output versus growth rate; points denote feasible retained draws and diamonds denote means. (b) Sum of absolute net reaction fluxes; boxes show the interquartile range, central lines the median, whiskers the full range and diamonds the mean. Dashed lines indicate imposed forward-plus-reverse total-flux caps, where applicable; this quantity differs from the net-flux sum plotted. (c) Empirical cumulative distributions of lag-1 Pearson correlations across varying net reaction fluxes. Reactions with a sampled range ≤10⁻⁷ are excluded; only adjacent valid draws contribute to correlations. Each condition uses one chain with a thinning interval of 100 transitions. Draws are correlated, and panel c is a mixing diagnostic rather than evidence of convergence or biological validation.

The target is the mass-weighted sum of hypothetical removal fluxes for all 343 neutral TAG species, with freely varying composition; it is model mass output, not experimentally validated secretion. All conditions retain the historical growth floor μ₀ = 0.14650760568106092 h⁻¹, which is not recalculated as 10% of the new maximum growth. Let μmax be maximum growth, qmax maximum TAG mass output with μ ≥ μ₀, and μslow the maximum growth with q ≥ 0.9 qmax. Growing uses μ ≥ max(μ₀, 0.9 μmax); Slow growth uses μ₀ ≤ μ ≤ μslow; Producing uses μ ≥ μ₀ and q ≥ 0.9 qmax. Growing and Slow growth additionally impose a cap equal to 1.25 times the minimum forward-plus-reverse total flux at their respective maximum growth. Producing has no additional total-flux cap.

Valid draws (Growing / Slow growth / Producing): 500 / 500 / 500. Reactions contributing to panel c: 2224 / 2366 / 1726.

Applied caps: Growing, 1223.1251; Slow growth, 300.44352.

Computed endpoints: μmax = 1.463512824 h⁻¹; qmax = 0.6067357658 g gDW⁻¹ h⁻¹; μslow = 0.2852106611 h⁻¹.
