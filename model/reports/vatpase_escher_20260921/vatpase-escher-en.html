Evidence limitations and next discriminating evidence

Growth: identify a measured acidification-dependent growth requirement under matched conditions and its mechanistic representation. ATP consumption by biomass alone does not establish a pump requirement.

Transport: establish physiological direction and energetic coupling for R1159. Its stored reaction permits reversible cytosol–Golgi H+ transfer without ATP; its saved WT flux is zero. No active bypass or unique explanation is established.

Identity and localization: resolve the W29 identity of the CLIB122 V1-F candidate, establish the two a candidates' native compartments, and test same-condition complementation. Resolve native shared-component dependency rather than infer it from desired essentiality labels.

Structure: previous one-copy AlphaFold branch inputs do not represent a validated native stoichiometric assembly. The A branch has audited raw files with limited inter-chain confidence; the B branch has only a webpage summary in the reviewed evidence. Predictions alone do not establish catalysis, localization or redundancy.
