## Gaps the auditor raised and what was done

1. **B11 misattribution, also in the model notes.** Hanaoka et al. 2026 name Tda5 as the DHRSX ortholog
   but no cofactor and no yeast enzyme for the dolichal step; the R_DOLSYN notes and the curation file
   credited them with Tda5 (NAD+), Dfg10 and Env9 (NADPH).
   - **Re-checked this session** (verified): Hanaoka abstract and full text; the Wilson 2026 preprint
     abstract (Env9, NADPH; Tda5 for polyprenol oxidation, no cofactor); and, to source the NAD+, the
     Wilson et al. 2024 Cell abstract (PMID 38821050: DHRSX "as a NAD+-dependent dehydrogenase and as a
     NADPH-dependent reductase"). First authors checked on PubMed and bioRxiv.
   - **Fixed:** the curation file's notes, `component_steps` and `evidence`; TASK.md section 12 (erratum;
     sections 1–9 left as approved); REPORT.md section 2. The candidate was rebuilt to
     `…_dolichol_route_v2_20261028.xml`, `9d655c2b…`, seeds 0 and 1 identical. Versus `a801aad0` the
     whole-model diff is the R_DOLSYN notes only, and the optimization problem is identical
     ([v2_same_lp.json](v2_same_lp.json)), so all LP results carry over. `a801aad0` is kept, superseded, with its data file (`7ba856df…`, regenerated byte for byte) in
     [superseded/](superseded/).
   - **What remains inferred:** the NAD+ of the yeast Tda5 step (from human DHRSX by orthology).
2. **YALI0 tags not on the KEGG pages.** The mapping source, the S2 locus table, is now named and its four
   rows were re-read (TASK.md section 12).
3. **pytest failures and errors not stated in TASK.md / STOP.md.** STOP.md and REPORT.md now give
   11 failed and 18 errors, the same tests before and after.
4. **Census provenance thin.** REPORT.md now gives the commands, the census-script SHAs and the fact that
   the `ce9e1112` census was reused from the Kennedy re-run.
5. **Wording.** STOP.md links AUDIT.md, which now exists; "11 passed" is stated as 6 dolichol + 5 R1726 tests.
6. **Pre-existing, not this change:** `m41` is named "NADP(+)_C21H28…" while its formula is C21H29; the
   curation file copies the model name. Not changed.
