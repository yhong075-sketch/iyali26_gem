## 2. Why (TASK.md sections 2–3; tiers as given there)

- **The route was split.** R2180 made dehydrodolichyl-PP and R287 phosphorylated dolichol, but nothing made
  dolichol. In between sat R2178, an iYli21 placeholder with no gene, name or annotation whose O2-burning
  equation matches no enzyme of the dolichol route (verified on the model; inferred from the sources read).
- **The biology** (verified from abstracts and articles; corrected after the audit, TASK.md section 12):
  - Human DHRSX oxidises polyprenol with NAD+ and reduces dolichal with NADPH; SRD5A3 reduces polyprenal in
    between (Wilson et al. 2024, PMID 38821050).
  - In *S. cerevisiae* Tda5 is the functional DHRSX ortholog (Hanaoka et al. 2026, PMID 42201967), Env9 reduces
    dolichal with NADPH (Wilson et al. 2026, bioRxiv preprint) and Dfg10 reduces polyprenal (KEGG R13444).
    The NAD+ of the yeast Tda5 step is inferred by orthology.
  - Sec59 phosphorylates dolichol to Dol-P and is essential. The CWH8 phosphatase recycles Dol-PP and is not
    essential.
- **Genes** (KEGG orthology, verified): `YALI1E22718g` = SEC59 ortholog K00902 (consensus-essential);
  `YALI1D26639g` = CWH8 ortholog K07252; DFG10 ortholog `YALI1E09357g` exists but is not in the model.
- **Why not a biomass term alone (Option A):** it makes R2178 obligatory and predicts CWH8 essential and SEC59
  dispensable, the reverse of the yeast genetics (Stage 1 LPs, verified).

  write a wrong model. Its one low finding (species of the new reaction not required to have identity rows,
  so a bad row would be found only after the first edit) and the untested guards were fixed before the
  builds.

### Two implementation findings (TASK.md section 10)

1. **2 H+, not the draft's 1.** The model writes NAD dehydrogenases without H+ and NADPH reductases with one
   H+ per NADPH ([stage1/proton_convention_scan.json](stage1/proton_convention_scan.json)). Written that
   way, the net has 2 H+. With a Dol-16 formula set it balances in every element, with charge −2, the
   model's NADP charge convention. Every LP result is identical for 1 and 2 H+.
2. **SBO annotation only.** EC 1.3.1.94 and KEGG R13444 describe only the Dfg10 step, so they are in the notes.

## 5. What it means

- **Network:** the dolichol route now runs as the yeast biology describes, dehydrodolichyl-PP → dolichol →
  Dol-P, instead of through an O2-burning placeholder. Dol-P is made and drained, not only cycled.
- **Essentiality:** SEC59 `YALI1E22718g` becomes a true positive (it is on the consensus list). The rer2 srt1
  double disruption is lethal in *S. cerevisiae*, and the double cis-prenyltransferase knockout is now lethal.
  `YALI1C26036g` (RER2 type, consensus-essential) stays a false negative through its OR partner. This change
  was not made to raise a score; the gain is one gene.
- **The gate:** the approved 1e-6 growth limit did not anticipate a WT shift. The restated gate (no class
  change except SEC59, no absolute growth gain) is what the user accepted; both parts hold.

## 6. Limits

- **Evidence:** orthology plus *S. cerevisiae* experiments; no *Y. lipolytica* dolichol data.
- **Chemistry:** R_DOLSYN adds 2 H+ while its charge does not close (−2 in the model's NADP convention).
  platform/AGENTS.md says no H+ is added unless H, O and charge all close; here the charge cannot close in
  this convention, and the 1 H+ form closes neither. Mass balance is not checkable until the dolichol-family
  formulas are set (a separate chemistry task).
- **Gene level:** R_DOLSYN is gene-less, so there is no prediction for the DFG10 / TDA5 / ENV9 orthologs. The
  NUS1 ortholog `YALI1F26726g` (consensus-essential) is not in the model.
- **Coefficient:** 1e-4 mmol/gDW is an order of magnitude from an *S. cerevisiae* figure with an assumed
  wet-to-dry conversion. Stage 1 showed growth and essentiality insensitive across 4e-5 to 1.6e-4.
- **Coverage:** only the Task A medium was tested.
- **Budget:** 5 builds against 3 planned (2 more after the notes correction). 262 check LPs against about 60 planned, counted from the scripts' loops
  (each FVA = 1 optimum + 2 per reaction): built checks 82 + 90, the 1 / 2 H+ comparison 2 × 26, the
  open-condition Gurobi FVA 2 × 19. The overrun is the route FVA at three fractions. Sweep (2,164) and census
  (2 scans) as planned.
- **Tooling, found in passing:** cobra on macOS rejects absolute SBML paths over 260 characters, so the
  checks and the sweep read the byte-identical seed-1 copy in the research folder. A libSBML notes error on
  R304 appears in every build log, including the one that made `ce9e1112`; not addressed.
