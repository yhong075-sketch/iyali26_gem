## 1. The problem

In `164f235f`:
- mtFAS (C4 → C8) carries no flux.
- No reaction makes a lipoyl cofactor. PDH (R693) has no lipoyl carrier, and OGDH and the
  glycine-cleavage steps only recycle one.
- So nothing requires mtFAS. Its genes, most of which are on the consensus essential list, could
  not be called essential.

A separate 5-LP in-memory test ([acp_block_test.json](evidence/acp_block_test.json),
[script](scripts/acp_block_test.py)) found that octanoyl-ACP has outlets. The block is the
mitochondrial ACP pool:
- **Octanoyl outlets:** they return the cytosolic `ACP` species (m1920), never ACP1 (m179).
- **ACP1:** has no synthesis.
- **The octanoyl export route** (R2216 → R2217, irreversible) has no way back.

| Test | Max of R1405 |
| --- | --- |
| as supplied | 0 |
| + ACP1 source only | 0 |
| + octanoyl-ACP sink only | 0 |
| + octanoyl-ACP → ACP1 | 4.74 |
| + cytosolic ACP → ACP1 | 13.6 (through R2216/R2217) |

Each octanoyl-ACP uses one ACP1 net (R78 −1, 3 × R539 −3, R1394–R1396 +3). This is provable.

**Design:**
- **Apo-protein cycle.** The biomass term returns the apo proteins, so only the lipoyl moiety is
  used up. The apo pools are closed and need no source.
- **Fluxes at steady state.** R2286 = R2287 = 2αμ and R2288 = αμ, where μ is the growth rate.
- **Existing carrier reused.** R2287 makes the dihydrolipoyl form (Rhea 16585), and the existing
  LPD1 step R364 oxidises it into the glycine-cleavage carrier.
- **Lipoyl-E2 is a growth-dilution token only.** It is not connected to the PDH (R693, lumped) or
  OGDH (R613/R614, free lipoamide) reactions.

## 3. Mechanism (code)

**Data:** [`model/curation/lipoylation/lipoylation_module.json`](../../curation/lipoylation/lipoylation_module.json),
generated by [scripts/make_lipoylation_spec.py](scripts/make_lipoylation_spec.py).

**Step:** `apply_lipoylation_module()` in `platform/scripts/gem_annotate/lipoylation_module.py`.
**Before any edit** it checks:
- every species formula, charge, compartment, and the `m586` name;
- the exact set of reactions that use ACP1 and lipoyl-H-protein;
- the residuals of all 11 ACP1 reactions against the recorded before-state;
- the R362/R364 definitions;
- that the new IDs are absent, plus the biomass terms and the context gene YALI1E00574g.

A partial or mixed state is rejected.

**After the edit** it re-verifies the whole after-state:
- the balance of all new and edited reactions, R78 and R539;
- the nine other ACP1 residuals against the record;
- that every lipoylation note and EC/Rhea annotation is present.

That last check would have caught the v1 notes loss.

**Wiring:** the flag `--lipoylation-module` is off by default and runs after the R1964 step.

**Tests:** `platform/tests/test_lipoylation_module.py` (5 tests, 8 subtests):
- only the targets change;
- rerun is a no-op, also after an SBML round trip, with notes present;
- stripped notes are rejected;
- eight stale or partial inputs, including residual drift on an ACP1 reaction, fail closed before
  any edit;
- a wrong scope is rejected;
- the flag is opt-in.

## 7. Chemistry notes

- **The nine ACP1 reactions still imbalanced.** These residuals pre-date the fill:

  | Reaction | Residual |
  | --- | --- |
  | R1394 | charge −1, H −1, R −1 |
  | R1395 | charge −1, H −3, C −2, O −1 (missing butanoyl- and 3-oxo-hexanoyl-ACP formulas) |
  | R1396 | charge −1, H −14, C −8, O −2, S −1 (missing 3-oxo-octanoyl-ACP formula) |
  | R2218 | charge −3, H −20, C −9, O −1, P +1, S −1, R −1, A +1 |
  | R2221, R2224, R2230 | charge −3, H −5, C −2 |
  | R2227 | charge −4, H −3, C +138, P −1, A −1 |
  | R2233 | charge −4, H −3, C +158, P −1, A −1 |

  They need their own chemistry review.
- **4 NADPH is a convention artefact.** With ChEBI charges the chemistry needs 2 NADPH (4
  electrons; audit-verified against ChEBI 18009/16474). The model's NADP(+) (m178) carries the
  cation formula C21H29N7O17P3 with charge 0, so each NADPH → NADP(+) here releases only one H.
  The charge-balanced form therefore needs 4.
  - The audit found 67 other NADPH reactions written with 1 H⁺ per NADPH and a −1 charge residual
    instead.
  - The cost is about 1.1e-4 NADPH per hour, which doesn't affect growth.
  - Fixing m178 would be a separate model-wide chemistry change.
- **Lumped donors.** The 2 inserted sulfur atoms are written as L-cysteine → L-alanine and the
  electrons as NADPH. Rhea's cluster accounting (2 H₂S + 4 Fe³⁺ released) is not represented.

## 8. R2216–R2241 review (no change)

- **R2216 and R2217:** R2216 is a gene-less mitochondrion → cytosol transport of octanoyl-ACP; its
  `FA120ACPtm` cross-reference is the C12 transporter. R2217 is an irreversible cytosolic
  hydrolysis assigned to FAS1/FAS2 (reciprocal P34229 FAS1_YARLI) with the whole FAS EC list.
- **Biology:** fungal type-I FAS carries its own ACP domain, and no export of acyl-ACP from
  mitochondria is known. (Established, not cited.)
- **Correction to my earlier claim that the whole block is blocked.** R2216 and R2218 stay at 0.
  But the reversible cytosolic thioesterases (R2226, R2229, R2232, R2235, R2237, R2239, R2241) and
  transports allow a C14 → C16 cycle:
  - cytosolic myristate → myristoyl-ACP;
  - mitochondrial elongation on ACP1 (R2230 with R539);
  - palmitate back in the cytosol.
  - The audit measured up to 36.389 through R2230 in **both** models, so it is pre-existing and
    unchanged by this module.

  This unsupported route should get its own review.
- **Warning:** connecting cytosolic ACP to ACP1 would also open the octanoyl export (13.6 in the
  ACP test).
