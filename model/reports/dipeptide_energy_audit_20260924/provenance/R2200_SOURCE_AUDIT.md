## Conflicts and interpretive limits

1. **Reference identity conflict is real but localized.** KEGG/historical RefSeq .2 use a DSM3286/YALI2 record, whereas .3/AOW use W29/YALI1. The one-Lys difference must stay explicit; the matching current AlphaFold model is correctly selected against W29. A current W29 sequence matching a CLIB122 protein also does not transfer organism-specific expression/localization or kinetics.
2. **Database status conflict is preserved.** AFDB still labels the entry reference-proteome while UniProt marks that accession inactive for reference-proteome exclusion. Exact sequence matching resolves reuse identity, not the database designation.
3. **2007 physiological growth and 2009 purified-enzyme results should not be conflated.** The earlier work inferred a broader di-/tripeptide role from growth and homology; the later direct enzyme panel gives narrower biochemical evidence. Neither tested the four non-Cys targets. This reinforces the report's refusal to fill four GPRs from DUG1/EC class alone.
4. **Structure is supportive only at the confidence/identity level in this package.** Since no independent structural functional comparison was run, the opening statement that functional support comes partly from AlphaFold should be read narrowly: a well-folded predicted structure of the correct sequence exists. The prediction itself adds no measured catalytic specificity or localization evidence.

## Decision

Accept the identity report as a **sequence-resolved, function-candidate audit** after explicitly retaining the historical .2 strain label. It is sufficient to nominate the protein for exact-substrate assays and localization tests; it is insufficient to add it to R2021/R2029/R2034/R2039 or migrate those reactions. R2200's existing reversible bound and missing Cys-Gly formula were checked as model facts, not biologically accepted by this audit.

Independent numeric checks and source file SHA256 values are saved in `R2200_independent_checks.json`. The reviewer parsed the raw sources directly rather than accepting `verify_identity.py` output or the report's conclusions. No new biological computation was claimed from fetching sources. Initial web source access failures are retained as limitations; no complete 2011 PDF or W29 native experimental evidence is claimed.
